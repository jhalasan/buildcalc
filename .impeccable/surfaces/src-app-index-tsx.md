## Surface

Buildcalc full Phase 1 app shell: Home, Calculators, calculator screen, Estimate, Settings. Adaptive Expo app. Mode: Operate. Replaces the ruled-paper "Site Fieldbook" world (user approved replacement).

## Audience and task

Philippine builders, foremen and owner-builders measuring work outdoors, often one-handed or gloved. They pick a work section, enter dimensions, check material quantities, type their own peso prices, and read Total Estimated Material Cost. Estimates are session-only; no accounts, saves, labor.

## Content and constraints

PRODUCT.md rules stand: no default prices, labor/equipment excluded line beside every total, wastage default zero and never on CHB, adjusted quantities tagged, planning-estimate notice. Six unbuilt sections shown as Planned, not tappable. Estimate tab holds in-session takeoffs only.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A pro site tool, not a worksheet: the live total rides with the thumb while inputs stay large; refuse the flat document-with-tabs look.

OWN-WORLD: Job-site yellow (#FFC21A) fields on charcoal and warm concrete grey; Barlow Condensed numerals, Barlow text; 14-20px radii, soft light-mode lift, tactile pressed states; one stenciled-line icon family.

STORY: See what is buildable, pick a section, key in measurements, watch quantities appear, price them, add to the estimate, read the total.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Charcoal masthead with yellow wordmark and current-estimate strip; two large work-section cards with icon tiles and calculator counts; Planned sections as a muted grid below; four-tab bar with yellow active pill.

FORM: User-pinned "Site-tool app" direction (no roll; seed none).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
