## Surface

Buildcalc concrete estimator; adaptive Expo screen. Mode: Operate.

## Audience and task

Audience is not yet specified. Select a footing, beam, column, slab, wall, or custom rectangular pour; enter metric dimensions, quantity, editable mix ratio, and cement/sand/aggregate prices in Philippine pesos (PHP); review material quantities and estimated materials cost. Slab height is thickness.

## Content and constraints

Gross rectangular concrete only; do not deduct wall openings or include labor or reinforcement. Default mix ratio 1:2:4 and dry-volume factor 1.54. Cement is reported and priced in 50 kg bags; sand and aggregate in m³. Surface assumptions must disclose cement density used for bag conversion and whole-bag rounding.
